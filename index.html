Frequently asked questions

Is home sample collection free?

Yes. Booking online and sample collection at your home are free. You only pay for the tests.

How will I get my report?

Reports are shared on WhatsApp and in the MicroCheck app. You can also book a free doctor and diet consultation to go through the results.

Do I need to fast before my test?

Some tests, such as fasting blood sugar and lipid profile, need 10 to 12 hours of fasting. Each package shows whether fasting is needed, and our team reminds you before the visit.

Which areas do you cover?

We collect samples in all 14 districts of Kerala. Choose your district at the top of the page.

Can I book for my family members?

Yes. Add tests to the cart and tell our team who each test is for when they confirm the booking.
